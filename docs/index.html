Hypothetica Studio

Hypothetica is an animated science channel on YouTube (@HypotheticaXYZ) that explores "What if...?" questions.

Hypothetica Studio is the channel's own internal production tool. It renders the channel's original animated videos and uploads them to the channel owner's own YouTube account, and reads that channel's own analytics to plan future videos. It is not offered to other users.
